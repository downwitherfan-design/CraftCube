# CraftCube v7.1 — World-Class Content Rules

## Story
Story must create curiosity, attachment and consequence through gameplay. Avoid exposition walls, branch explosions and disposable characters.

## Characters
Every major character needs a worldview, desire, conflict, change and payoff. Every companion needs a gameplay role plus emotional identity.

## Items
Every important item must have identity, provenance, function, interaction and a reason to remember it.

## Equipment
Equipment should produce choices. Prefer capability, context and sidegrades over stat escalation.

## Upgrades
A strong upgrade changes possibilities or consistency rather than simply making old puzzles obsolete.

## Quests
Every quest needs purpose and consequence. Repeatables must offer mastery, discovery or changing states.

## World
A biome needs a mechanical identity and an emotional identity. Environment, audio, creatures and story should reinforce the same theme.

## Discovery
Secrets must be discoverable through observation, experimentation or mastery, never arbitrary guessing.

## Content density
Use a “fewer but memorable” rule. A smaller number of iconic items, characters, places and events is preferred to a huge catalog of shallow content.
