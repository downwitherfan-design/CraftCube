# CraftCube v7 — Progression & Mastery Bible

## Progression layers

### Layer 1 — Knowledge
The player learns puzzle rules and world language.

### Layer 2 — Capability
New equipment, companions and interaction abilities expand options.

### Layer 3 — Access
New biomes, routes and mysteries become available.

### Layer 4 — Mastery
The player can optimize, speedrun, minimize moves or solve advanced variants.

## Mastery metrics

Possible metrics:
- completion,
- move efficiency,
- optional assist usage,
- solve consistency,
- challenge completion,
- discovery completion.

Do not collapse mastery into a single score if the dimensions tell different stories.

## Reward hierarchy

Prefer:
1. new knowledge,
2. meaningful new capability,
3. world change,
4. narrative payoff,
5. collection value,
6. cosmetic expression.

Raw currency should be lower priority.

## Revisit design

Revisiting old content is valuable when a new capability creates a genuinely different interaction or reveals a secret.
It is not acceptable to force replay merely to inflate playtime.

## Challenge types

- Standard solve
- Limited assistance
- Move efficiency
- Time challenge
- World-state challenge
- Resonance challenge
- Discovery challenge
- Companion challenge

## Difficulty curve

Difficulty should primarily increase through understanding and decision quality before raw speed.

## Endgame

Endgame should be a mastery layer:
- deterministic challenge seeds,
- advanced variants,
- rare discovery routes,
- collection completion,
- optional world completion states.
