## Design test for every approved item

1. What does the player actually do differently?
2. Why is this more interesting than a stat boost?
3. Which puzzle/world relationship does it strengthen?
4. Does it create a meaningful decision?
5. What is the story meaning?
6. Can the item be taught without a lore wall?
7. Can it be tested deterministically?
8. Does it remain understandable on a phone screen?
