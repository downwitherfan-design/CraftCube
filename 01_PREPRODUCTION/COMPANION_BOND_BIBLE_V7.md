# CraftCube v7 — Companion & Character Bond Bible

## Companion philosophy

Companions are not passive cosmetics and not generic combat pets.

They should contribute through:
- sensing,
- reacting,
- world interaction,
- contextual hints,
- emotional connection,
- discovery,
- or specialized support.

## Bond progression

Meet -> Trust -> Shared discovery -> Capability -> Memory -> Choice

## Bond must not become mandatory grinding

Companions should deepen the game for interested players without blocking the central puzzle path.

## Companion reactions

A companion may react to:
- specific puzzle states,
- biome health,
- important relics,
- story revelations,
- successful mastery moments,
- player choices.

## Character arcs

Each major companion/character should have:
- starting belief,
- personal conflict,
- relationship with the Loom,
- turning point,
- payoff.

## Relationship UI

Keep it readable and story-first. Avoid a spreadsheet-like affinity screen.
