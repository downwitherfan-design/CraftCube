# CraftCube v7 — Quest & Discovery Bible

## Quest philosophy

Quests are reasons to care about a place or problem, not checklists of chores.

## Quest layers

### Main quests
Move the central story forward.

### Companion quests
Reveal a relationship, memory or capability.

### World quests
Restore, investigate or stabilize local systems.

### Discovery activities
Optional mysteries, secret states and environmental puzzles.

## Quest structure

HOOK -> CONTEXT -> PLAYER ACTION -> CONSEQUENCE -> REVELATION/REWARD

## Discovery categories

- visual secret,
- puzzle-state secret,
- environmental secret,
- companion secret,
- item-memory secret,
- alternate world state,
- mastery secret.

## Anti-spoiler design

Discoveries should offer breadcrumbs rather than direct solutions.

## Replayability

Use deterministic challenge seeds and state-based discovery rather than uncontrolled random loot.

## Secret design test

Every secret should answer:
- Why would the player notice it?
- What skill rewards it?
- What does it reveal?
- Is it worth the effort?
