# CraftCube v7 — Item & Equipment Progression

## Purpose
Items and equipment deepen exploration, puzzle strategy, narrative identity and player expression. They are not a generic RPG stat layer.

## Item families
1. Story Relics — lore and world significance.
2. Tools — enable or alter specific world interactions.
3. Resonance Equipment — interacts with puzzle/world states through affinity.
4. Companion Items — influence companion utility or behavior.
5. Resources — clear crafting/repair/activation inputs and sinks.
6. Collectibles — discovery, lore, cosmetics, completion or world-memory value.

## Equipment philosophy
Prefer revealing, stabilizing, interpreting, redirecting, traversing, interacting, or opportunity-creating effects over raw stat inflation.

## Tradeoffs
Strong effects can use charges, cooldowns, resource costs, affinity limits or opportunity costs where they improve decisions. Tradeoffs must remain clear.

## Puzzle Affinity
Equipment can have data-driven affinities linking it to puzzle states, challenge categories, biomes, or world conditions. Affinity creates options; it never removes core puzzle mastery.

## Upgrades
Upgrades should unlock meaningful behavior, reliability, versatility or expression—not endless +1 stat inflation.

## Acquisition
Important items may be earned through story, exploration, mastery, secrets, world restoration, companions or optional challenges. Critical progression should not rely on blind random drops.

## Inventory
Keep the inventory intentionally compact, readable, fast to navigate and easy to recover from accidental actions.

## Save safety
Item IDs, quantities, equipped state, charges/durability, upgrades, discovery state and ownership must be versioned and migration-safe.

## Balance guard
Low-investment players must remain able to progress using puzzle skill. Equipment expands options rather than creating grind-to-win gates.
