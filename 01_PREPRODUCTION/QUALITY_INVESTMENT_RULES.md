# Quality + Investment Protection Rules

1. Quality over speed.
2. Validate high-risk assumptions before expensive implementation.
3. Prefer reversible experiments before irreversible production work.
4. Do not build a feature merely because it is technically interesting.
5. Do not optimize visual spectacle at the expense of readability, touch quality or performance.
6. Do not optimize engineering elegance at the expense of player value.
7. Every major feature needs: player value, technical cost, performance impact, QA plan, and cut criteria.
8. Every phase must end with measurable evidence, not optimism.
9. A beautiful vertical slice outranks large unfinished content volume.
10. If quality is below the project bar, expand the phase rather than lowering the bar.
