# CraftCube — Investment Gate (Pre-Phase 00)

## Purpose
Protect time, money and production effort before full parallel development begins.

## Rule
No large-scale content production, monetization implementation, or irreversible technical commitment is allowed until this gate is passed.

## Gate A — Product viability
- One-sentence value proposition is clear.
- Core loop is explainable in under 30 seconds.
- Target audience is explicit.
- Differentiators are documented.
- Core fantasy and puzzle→world→story relationship are clear.
- Story premise and item/equipment philosophy are documented.
- Discovery/replayability hypothesis is documented.
- Top 5 direct/adjacent competitors are reviewed.
- Feature list is split into Must / Should / Later / Reject.

## Gate B — Technical feasibility
- Android-first architecture is viable.
- Target device tiers are defined.
- Rendering approach and performance budgets are plausible.
- Save, content and puzzle systems can operate offline.
- Build pipeline can produce a clean test APK.

## Gate C — Experience proof
A small playable prototype or equivalent interactive proof must demonstrate:
- Core interaction feels good.
- Puzzle readability is good.
- Touch input is reliable.
- Feedback is satisfying.
- Visual direction is strong enough to justify the planned art investment.
- At least one story beat creates curiosity without a lore wall.
- At least one meaningful equipment loop improves the core experience without trivializing mastery.
- At least one discovery loop demonstrates optional replay value.

## Gate D — Business protection
- Monetization hypothesis is documented, even if the game launches premium or ad-free.
- No feature is included solely because a competitor has it.
- Scope has a hard release target and a cut list.
- Third-party/IP/licensing risks are identified.

## Gate decision
PASS = continue to Phase 00.
CONDITIONAL = continue only with explicit mitigation owners and dates.
FAIL = stop expansion and revise product direction before spending on production.

## Evidence required
Decision record, competitor notes, prototype evidence, technical feasibility notes, scope/cut list, risk register.
