# CraftCube v7 — Story & Living World Vision

## Narrative promise
CraftCube is a story-driven puzzle adventure in which the player restores a living world whose physical laws are expressed through a twisting spatial puzzle. The story is discovered through play, world changes, characters, artifacts, audio, and consequences—not only exposition.

## Narrative pillars
- Mystery before exposition.
- World tells the story.
- Player choices can alter meaningful local outcomes without fracturing production scope.
- Every chapter reveals part of the world's truth and adds a meaningful form of puzzle/world causality.
- Emotional clarity matters even when deeper lore remains mysterious.

## Three-act structure
Act I — Awakening: discover the world, the cube, the first companion, and the first signs of instability.
Act II — Fracture: travel across distinct biomes, discover the cause and cost of instability, and acquire equipment that creates new forms of interaction.
Act III — Reconciliation: master the puzzle/world language, resolve the central conflict, and determine the final state of the world.

The exact story, names, characters, factions, and terminology are owned by AI-09 and must be original/IP-safe.

## Narrative cadence
Hook → curiosity → first interaction → escalation → discovery → consequence → mastery beat → payoff → new question.

## World memory
Meaningful actions create persistent data-driven states such as restored, altered, discovered, corrupted, mastered, or unresolved. These may affect later presentation, dialogue, optional encounters, rewards, traversal, or lore.

## No lore walls
Critical gameplay understanding must never depend on reading long text. Use environmental cues, short dialogue, animation, audio, and interactive discovery.

## Secrets
Some secrets should require a combination of puzzle behavior, world observation, equipment and exploration. Secrets must remain optional and testable.
