# CraftCube v7 — Character & Companion System

## Character principles
Characters are designed around function + personality + change. Avoid a large cast with no meaningful arc.

## Companion roles
A companion can provide one or more of: emotional anchor, contextual clue, discovery sensor, optional utility, world interaction, equipment synergy, or narrative mirror.

## Relationship progression
Relationship growth should come from shared gameplay and discoveries, not only a bar that fills after arbitrary tasks.

## Companion reaction model
Puzzle result + world state + story state + equipped context may produce a companion reaction event. Reaction can be visual, audio, dialogue, behavior, or discovery.

## Avoid combat bloat
Companions do not require combat systems simply to justify their existence.

## Arc payoff
Each important companion should have at least one setup, one meaningful change, and one payoff in the planned content scope.
