# CraftCube v7 — Discovery & Replayability Framework

## Discovery layers
- Surface: obvious discoveries for newcomers.
- Curious: clue-driven optional discoveries.
- Mastery: precise puzzle performance reveals rare states.
- Systemic: puzzle + world + equipment combinations create emergent discoveries.

## Replayability sources
Mastery, alternate routes, secrets, challenge variants, collection, world-state observation and optional story discoveries. Replayed content must be skippable where story repetition would become friction.

## Determinism
Secrets must be deterministic or use controlled, testable seeds. They must never create irreversible progression blockers.

## Discovery ledger
Persist discovered locations, events, relics, creatures, lore entries, puzzle phenomena and world changes.

## Anti-spoiler UX
Signal that something is discoverable without immediately revealing the solution.
