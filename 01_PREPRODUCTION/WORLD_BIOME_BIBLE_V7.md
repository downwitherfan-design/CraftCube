# CraftCube v7 — Living World & Biome Bible

## World design rule

Biomes are not skins. Each biome is a different expression of the puzzle/world relationship.

## Biome 1 — Hearthwild

Theme: warmth, restoration, first growth.
Puzzle relationship: orientation, basic alignment.
World response: paths, water channels, plant growth, safe creature habitats.
Narrative function: teach the player that solving changes reality.

## Biome 2 — Glassroot

Theme: crystal growth and memory.
Puzzle relationship: pattern recognition and state echoes.
World response: crystal structures reveal previous states and hidden routes.
Narrative function: introduce world memory.

## Biome 3 — Sunken Grid

Theme: flooded ruins and lost infrastructure.
Puzzle relationship: sequencing and controlled restoration.
World response: pumps, bridges, elevators, light networks.
Narrative function: reveal contradictions in historical records.

## Biome 4 — Ember Hollow

Theme: heat, unstable energy, transformation.
Puzzle relationship: risk/reward and controlled instability.
World response: heat channels, forge-like structures, temporary routes.
Narrative function: teach that instability is not always failure.

## Biome 5 — Verdant Archive

Theme: living archive, giant roots, biological memory.
Puzzle relationship: long-range pattern planning.
World response: organic structures encode clues and react to mastery.
Narrative function: deepen the mystery of the Loom.

## Biome 6 — The Quiet Engine

Theme: ancient machine environment.
Puzzle relationship: advanced multi-state interaction.
World response: architecture literally rearranges around solved configurations.
Narrative function: expose the Loom's intended purpose.

## World-state model

A biome may contain:
- restored state,
- unstable state,
- discovered state,
- mastered state,
- story state,
- companion state,
- optional secret state.

All persistent state must be explicit and save-safe.

## World Memory principle

The player should be able to look back and see evidence of what they did. Do not make every consequence huge. Small persistent changes are often more believable and affordable.

## Biome transformation tiers

Tier 0 — dormant
Tier 1 — awakened
Tier 2 — recovering
Tier 3 — flourishing
Tier 4 — mastered/rare state

Do not force every biome to reach Tier 4 through mandatory grinding.
