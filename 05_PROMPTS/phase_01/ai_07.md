## CraftCube v7 — Story / World / Item / Equipment Addendum

This phase must preserve independent ownership and parallel execution while supporting the v7 product layer. Apply only responsibilities owned by this account.

### Non-negotiable
- Puzzle mastery remains the core skill.
- Story, world, companions, items and equipment deepen the puzzle/world identity; they do not turn the game into a generic RPG or Minecraft clone.
- Use contracts, mocks and fixtures instead of waiting on another AI.
- Do not invent IDs, fields, files, tests or implementation facts.
- Cross-domain changes require versioned contracts and evidence.

### Story
Use gameplay, world changes, characters, discoveries, environmental clues, audio and concise dialogue. Avoid lore dumps. Narrative state must be deterministic/testable where required and save-safe.

### Items / Equipment
Prefer meaningful behavior, exploration, strategy, world interaction and narrative value over raw stat inflation. Equipment may assist but must not remove protected puzzle mastery.

### Discovery / World Memory
Support optional, testable secrets and persistent meaningful state. Avoid progression blockers and uncontrolled randomness.

### Handoff
When this phase changes story/item/world behavior, report owner, contract version, inputs, outputs, stable IDs, fixtures, evidence, limitations, integration step and rollback step.

### Account-specific emphasis
UX: accessible inventory/equipment/discovery/story flows with low cognitive load and strong mobile ergonomics.
