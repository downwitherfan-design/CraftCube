# CraftCube v7 — Exact Start Sequence

## Day-one sequence

### Step 1 — Create the canonical repository
Use the project repository as the only source of truth for implementation work.

### Step 2 — Provision 13 independent AI workspaces
Each account receives:
- the same repository baseline,
- its own branch/workspace,
- its account track,
- the Master Control Prompt,
- the current phase prompt.

### Step 3 — Run Phase 00 in parallel
All 13 accounts perform their Phase 00 work independently.

They are allowed to disagree.
They are not allowed to hide disagreements.

### Step 4 — Collect evidence
AI-13 aggregates:
- proposals,
- contract changes,
- risks,
- unresolved conflicts,
- prototype evidence,
- scope recommendations.

### Step 5 — Human Product Decision
The human product authority approves or rejects major identity/story/scope decisions.

### Step 6 — Freeze contracts
Only after design decisions are resolved should the relevant contracts become RELEASED.

### Step 7 — Phase 01 onward
Continue parallel execution using mocks wherever implementation dependencies are incomplete.

### Step 8 — Investment Gate
Do not spend heavily on content until the Gold Slice proves the unique fantasy.

## Important

The goal is not for all 13 AI outputs to look identical.
The goal is for their outputs to be **compatible and combinable**.

Different specialists are expected to optimize different dimensions while respecting the same contracts and product pillars.
