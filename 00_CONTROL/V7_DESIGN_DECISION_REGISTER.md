# CraftCube v7 — Design Decision Register

These are **starting design decisions**, not irreversible facts. Phase 00 may revise them through the normal decision-control process.

## DD-001 — Puzzle is the protected core
The game must remain fun when story/meta/equipment are temporarily disabled.

## DD-002 — Equipment changes possibilities, not puzzle truth
Equipment can reveal, access, assist, redirect, interact or create tradeoffs. It must not secretly alter the mathematical truth of the core puzzle.

## DD-003 — Story is gameplay-first
Canon should be discoverable through action, world changes, characters, objects, sound and concise dialogue.

## DD-004 — World remembers meaningful moments
Important restoration, corruption, discoveries, companion moments and story consequences persist where practical and safe.

## DD-005 — Fewer, stronger items
Avoid hundreds of filler items. Major items should be memorable and useful.

## DD-006 — Progression must never depend on luck alone
Critical story/capability progression always has a deterministic path.

## DD-007 — Companions add meaning, not combat bloat
Companions focus on sensing, discovery, interaction, emotional attachment and contextual support unless later evidence proves another role valuable.

## DD-008 — Scoped choices, not branch explosion
Player decisions can affect presentation, local world state, dialogue, optional routes, memories and finale nuance without creating unmaintainable content trees.

## DD-009 — Healthy retention
Return value comes from mastery, curiosity, world change, collection, companions, secrets and new puzzle language; not artificial waiting.

## DD-010 — Android-first reality
Performance, memory and touch quality are product requirements from Phase 0 onward.

## DD-011 — Coherence over quantity
A smaller number of memorable, connected systems is better than broad shallow content.

## DD-012 — Equipment creates choices, not invalidation
Equipment and upgrades should expand possibility and strategy without making puzzle mastery irrelevant.

## DD-013 — Characters remember selectively
Narrative memory uses a controlled flag set so the world can feel responsive without branch explosion.

## DD-014 — Economy must never become a delay machine
Resources exist only when they improve preparation, discovery, choice or progression.

## DD-015 — Offline core first
Analytics, leaderboards and remote content must never be required for the core offline experience.

## DD-016 — World-class means memorable
Features are retained when players can understand their purpose and plausibly remember them beyond the session.
