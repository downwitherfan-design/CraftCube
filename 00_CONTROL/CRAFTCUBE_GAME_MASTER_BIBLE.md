# CraftCube v7 — Game Master Bible

## 1. Product identity

CraftCube is an original mobile puzzle-adventure in which the player restores and reshapes a living world by mastering a physical twisting puzzle language.

The game may evoke the freedom and wonder of a block-building sandbox, but it is **not** a Minecraft clone and it must not depend on copying Minecraft's art, creatures, terminology, UI or progression patterns.

## 2. Core fantasy

**"I am learning the language of a mysterious living cube, and every time I master it, the world answers me."**

## 3. Protected gameplay hierarchy

1. Puzzle mastery
2. Puzzle/world causality
3. Exploration and discovery
4. Story and character attachment
5. Meaningful equipment choices
6. Expression and collection
7. Optional services/monetization

Lower layers can never invalidate the higher layers.

## 4. Core loop

`Observe -> Manipulate -> Understand -> Solve/Adapt -> World Responds -> Discover -> Choose Reward -> Equip/Upgrade -> Revisit or Advance`

## 5. Emotional targets

The player should repeatedly feel:
- curiosity,
- tactile satisfaction,
- "I figured it out",
- visible consequence,
- discovery,
- attachment,
- anticipation,
- mastery.

## 6. Story premise

The world is built around the **Axis Loom**, an ancient spatial mechanism whose shifting geometry once kept the world's regions in harmony. A catastrophic event called the **Great Misalignment** fractured that harmony. The player awakens as a **Weaver**, a person capable of reading and manipulating the Loom's cube-like language.

The player is not simply collecting power. They are learning why the world broke, why the Loom responds to them, and whether restoring the old order is actually the right answer.

## 7. The world's central mystery

The visible crisis is the fractured world. The deeper mystery is that the Loom may not have failed by accident. Evidence gradually suggests that its creators deliberately built a mechanism capable of changing the rules of the world when enough mastery was achieved.

This creates a three-layer mystery:
- What happened?
- Who caused it?
- What was the Loom actually designed to become?

## 8. Player progression fantasy

The player's growth is expressed through:
- better puzzle mastery,
- understanding world rules,
- new equipment capabilities,
- stronger companion bonds,
- deeper environmental access,
- more difficult challenge variants,
- and meaningful story knowledge.

Raw numerical inflation is secondary.

## 9. Equipment philosophy

Every important equipment piece should answer at least two of:
- Does it change how I interact with puzzles?
- Does it change what I can discover?
- Does it change how I interact with the world?
- Does it connect to my companion?
- Does it reveal story?
- Does it create a meaningful build choice?

## 10. World philosophy

The world should remember meaningful events. Restoration, corruption, discoveries, companion moments and important puzzle outcomes may persist and visibly alter later exploration.

## 11. Healthy retention

Return motivation comes from:
- unexplored places,
- unresolved mysteries,
- mastery challenges,
- meaningful collection,
- world changes,
- companion progression,
- optional secrets,
- new puzzle languages.

No dark-pattern dependency.

## 12. Non-goals

Do not become:
- a generic RPG,
- a pure Rubik simulator,
- a block-building clone,
- a loot-grind game,
- a stat treadmill,
- a menu-heavy collection game,
- an online-required experience.

## 13. Quality definition

A global-quality release must be:
- immediately understandable,
- mechanically satisfying,
- visually distinctive,
- emotionally coherent,
- responsive on touch devices,
- stable under interruption,
- performant across Android tiers,
- and deep enough to reward mastery.

## 14. World-class production rule

CraftCube is judged by coherence and memorability, not feature count. Story, characters, items, equipment, quests, biomes, audio and progression must reinforce the same player fantasy. New complexity requires evidence of player value.
