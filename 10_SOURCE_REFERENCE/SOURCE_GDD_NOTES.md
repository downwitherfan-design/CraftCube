# Source GDD Reference Notes

The supplied ZIP was reviewed as the product source. Its major concepts include:
- cube/twisting puzzle gameplay
- biome-driven world
- Vitality and Bloom/Collapse
- special pieces/tiles
- companions
- environmental interactions
- Smart Assist
- Cube Pulse / Golden Trail
- multiple chapters / three-act dimensional progression
- Unity 6 + URP architectural direction
- pure C# simulation as a target
- data-driven content
- mobile performance budgets

The source also uses Minecraft-specific names, creatures and presentation. For an independent commercial game, keep the **mechanical design ideas** but separate them from proprietary Minecraft branding/assets unless a licensed path is explicitly chosen.

This note is intentionally a reference, not a replacement for legal advice.
