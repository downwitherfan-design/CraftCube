## What's in this build

EPIC 0.4 closes the "first ten minutes" experience and gives the world a voice. All changes are
backward compatible; an existing save is loaded unchanged.

- **Gameplay audio (Milestone E).** A new `AudioDirector` synthesises seven short cues locally
  (no external assets): a legal turn, a refused/locked move, a first solve, world restoration,
  a discovery, a world choice, and Nox's awakening. Cues are built once into a bounded player
  pool and are safe under the headless/Dummy audio driver. A real, persisted mute is available
  from both the Settings screen and the gameplay HUD; the obsolete "no audio implemented" note
  is gone.
- **In-world item loop (Milestone F).** A procedural cache now appears in the restored area once
  the seed bed is solved. Tapping it is the genuine item discovery: it grants the Stone Lens and
  records the discovery exactly once. While the Stone Lens is carried, a previously blank slab
  (the veiled mark) becomes tappable; using the lens reveals its hidden glyph meshes in the
  world — a real, visible change, recorded exactly once and restored from the save on reload.
- Two original prototype discoveries accompany the loop: *The Stone Lens* and *The Veiled Glyph*.

This builds on the EPIC 0.4 A–D work already on the branch: the single interaction arbiter,
first-run guidance with a compact mobile HUD and non-interrupting first solve, distinct
persistent world reactions with the sigil clue, and the scoped world choice.

## Known limitations

- Debug-signed with a development keystore; **not** a production/store release.
- No physical Android device was used; the APK was built and statically verified in CI.
- The APK is produced by the pipeline and attached as a release asset; it is not committed to source.

## Policy

attached to a GitHub Release; an ordinary CI pass is not the release deliverable.
